ABOUT SSDS

Building voices. Shaping futures.

SSDS is dedicated to helping students become confident speakers, critical thinkers, and passionate leaders through debate.

Our Story What We Do

OUR STORY

Giving students a voice.

SSDS was created with the goal of making debate accessible, engaging, and rewarding for students of all experience levels.

We believe that debate is more than simply winning an argument. It is an opportunity for students to learn how to think critically, communicate effectively, and understand different perspectives.

Through practices, workshops, tournaments, and other programs, SSDS gives students the opportunity to develop these skills in a supportive and engaging environment.

Whether someone is completely new to debate or already competing at a high level, our goal is to help every student improve and find confidence in their own voice.

WHAT WE DO

Debate that goes beyond the classroom.

We provide students with opportunities to learn, practice, compete, and connect with other debaters.

01

Workshops

Learn debate fundamentals, argumentation, rebuttal, research, public speaking, and other essential skills.

02

Tournaments

Put your skills into practice by competing in tournaments and experiencing the excitement of competitive debate.

03

Community

Meet other students who share an interest in debate, public speaking, and critical thinking.

04

Leadership

Develop the confidence and leadership skills needed to make an impact inside and outside the debate room.
